# GSM Nutrition Centre Website

## Experience
- Build a single, continuous premium wellness landing page at `/` with warm ivory, natural greens, muted earth tones, and restrained citrus accents.
- Use oversized editorial typography, overlapping compositions, organic shapes, soft background transitions, and generous whitespace rather than boxed sections or repetitive grids.
- Add a transparent-to-solid sticky navigation, polished mobile menu, smooth anchor navigation, refined entry and scroll motion, and reduced-motion support.

## Page flow
1. Cinematic full-screen hero with an art-directed wellness placeholder, layered headline, two calls to action, and scroll cue.
2. Flowing goal introduction and three connected asymmetric goal panels for weight management, healthy weight gain, and everyday wellness.
3. Overlapping nutrition composition with visual labels and ingredient-inspired details.
4. Curved, scroll-reactive four-step journey that becomes a vertical timeline on mobile.
5. Human guidance composition with coach/centre placeholder and three supporting principles.
6. Three visually varied nutrition solution areas with replaceable imagery.
7. Editorial Google review placeholders showing only the supplied public rating and review count, without fabricated testimonials.
8. Immersive final call to action flowing into location details, map placeholder, and a minimal footer.

## Reusable system
- Create reusable navigation, button, image-placeholder, section-label, animated-text, goal-panel, and timeline pieces.
- Centralize the supplied palette, typography, shapes, motion, and responsive behavior in the design system.
- Keep every visual placeholder ratio-safe with cover cropping so real business photography can replace it later.

## Technical details
- Implement with React, TanStack Router, Tailwind CSS v4, semantic HTML, and lightweight browser-native scroll/intersection animation.
- Keep motion smooth and restrained; lighten it on mobile and disable it for reduced-motion preferences.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph, and social metadata.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile widths, including navigation, anchor links, overflow, and the timeline behavior.
